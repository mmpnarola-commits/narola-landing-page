3. Found Node v18.12.1 was below the minimum for Next.js 14 (`>=18.17.0`), 15

    `create-next-app`'s `--agents-md` flag (which only affects the initial scaffold). Read
    the relevant Next.js source (`generate-agent-files.js`) to confirm behavior.

19. Did not commit or push any changes.
